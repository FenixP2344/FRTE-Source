# SWAT ELITE FORCE: FIRST RESPONDERS Tactical Edition #

The SEF First Responders Tactical Edition is a team of players from the Chinese SWAT 4 community who have carefully crafted it based on SEF First Responders and attempted to balance the advantages of other SWAT4 MODs. This MOD has made significant modifications to gameplay, balance, ROE, and the threat level of suspect AI, making it more competitive (classic mode) and entertaining (Terrorist Hunting mode) in gameplay.

If you would like to get more infro of FRTE, you're welcome to join in the SEF:FRTE Discord Channel: [Discord.gg](https://discord.gg/prbqaR9q). Thanks for the patiently wait, hope u guys enjoy, please feel free to leave your precious comments and feedback to us.

Introduction ThreatLevel System:
The Suspect will be classified into three levels of threat based on different Mission
 - Low threat: The suspects have low attack desire and weak combat ability, and players need to Following Standard ROE
 - Middle Threat: The suspect has a strong desire to attack, with moderate or high combat ability. In most cases, players need to Following the ROE,But in the event of potential threats Authorize the use of Deadly force
 - High Threat: The suspect equipped with RPG or grenade launchers and suicide vests, Authorized of deadly force at any time

Players need to determine the suspect's threat level based on the information provided in the briefing:
 - Ordinary type suspects are classified as low threat (such as gang members, bank robbers, or other conventional suspects)
 - Having received professional training (such as mercenaries, veterans/active duty soldiers, Mafia, army dealers, drug dealers) is listed as a medium threat level
 - In addition to possessing high-risk weapons, suspects in certain special levels are also listed as high threat (such as PLP members on the map "Carlyle", the mission of taking the Russian President Stomas hostage)

Note: Due to the mixed use of CharacterTypes and Spawngroup on unofficial maps, this threatlevel system is not applicable to other unofficial maps
